# Devon Labs portfolio

An open editorial portfolio inspired by rsms.me, styled with the Strider design system from the sibling project's DESIGN.md and rendered boards.devonlabs.space interface. Strider's requested hostname did not resolve during reference inspection.

Desktop: compact greeting, vertical navigation with a titanium marker, then three parallel columns: software; film and experiments; personal introduction and photography. Project lists remain unframed. The sidebar's two outbound actions use opaque panels. At tablet widths the personal sidebar becomes a two-column introductory row; mobile places the personal introduction first, then software, film, photography.

Canvas #efece6, graphite dark canvas #151617, titanium text #17181b, dark text #f2f0ea, 18px faint dot grid. Muted light text #62676d is slightly stronger than Strider's token for readable small copy. Space Grotesk headings, local Inter body, local Monaspace technical metadata. Corners: 8px action panels, 3px film image. Motion is limited to action hover and anchor scrolling; reduced motion is respected.

Theme follows the OS until manually overridden, then persists locally. Native anchor navigation, external repositories, public photography, camp film, hardware shop, and the retained /workshop/ route must remain accessible. Do not invent writing archives, metrics, credentials, or product claims.

The generated concept is a layout target. Its invented descriptions and technology stacks must be corrected to match data/developer-projects.ts; photographs and film imagery must use real existing assets.
